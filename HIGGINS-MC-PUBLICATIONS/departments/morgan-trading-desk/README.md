# Morgan Trading Desk Publications

Morgan Trading Desk is the Higgins Mission Control department for prediction markets, trading-system research, financial AI, quantitative decision support, market microstructure, portfolio risk, and trading-agent evaluation.

**Publication prefix:** `MOR`

Future publications may include market-structure studies, model and strategy evaluations, trading-agent architectures, risk frameworks, research digests, and independently reviewable performance analyses. Every public report must distinguish simulation, backtesting, paper trading, and live performance. It must state assumptions, uncertainty, costs, and material limitations.

Public editions must not expose credentials, exchange keys, private account data, live positions, non-public signals, exploitable infrastructure details, or misleading performance claims.
