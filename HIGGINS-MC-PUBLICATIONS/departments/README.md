# Higgins MC Publication Departments

This directory separates publications by the department that owns the subject-matter expertise. Higgins Mission Control remains the umbrella and governance layer for the complete library.

| Code | Department | Publication scope |
|---|---|---|
| `JLC` | [Justitia Legal Council](justitia-legal-council/README.md) | Law, compliance, legal operations, legal AI, and governance |
| `MOR` | [Morgan Trading Desk](morgan-trading-desk/README.md) | Trading systems, prediction markets, financial AI, market intelligence, and risk |
| `ELON` | [Team Elon — Technology Department](team-elon-technology/README.md) | Platform engineering, AI infrastructure, cybersecurity, emerging technology, and architecture |

New departments must define a unique code and English publication scope before their first publication is indexed.
